Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mini Games ("we", "us") handles information when you visit this website and play our games. By using the site, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

You do not need an account to play. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details, and we do not sell personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

Our games use your browser's local storage to remember things such as best scores, game progress, quiz results, your sound on/off setting and whether you have seen a tutorial. This data stays in your browser on your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data for this website.

3. Analytics

We may use analytics services, such as Google Analytics, to understand how many people visit and which games they play (for example, page views, game starts and scores). These services may use cookies or similar technologies and collect information such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you visit. We use this information only in aggregate to improve our games. You can learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Advertising

To keep our games free, we may display advertisements served by third-party vendors, including Google.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we will ask for your consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used.

5. Hosting and third-party services

Some game code libraries are loaded from public content delivery networks (CDNs). Like any website, these providers receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests files from them. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security purposes.

6. Children's privacy

Our games are suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold user accounts, most data can be removed simply by clearing your browser data. For any other request, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@myminigamesbar.com or visit our Contact page.
