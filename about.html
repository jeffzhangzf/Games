About Mini Games

Free games for a quick break.

Mini Games is a small collection of free, lightweight games that run right in your web browser. There is nothing to download or install and no account to create — open a game and start playing on your phone, tablet or computer.

Our games

  • Number Merge — drop number balls and merge matching ones into bigger numbers.
  • Two Beat One — a classic folk board game played against an AI opponent.
  • Balloon Pop — pop as many balloons as you can in 60 seconds.
  • Gamer Personality Quiz — 10 questions to find out what kind of gamer you are.
  • Idle Farm — grow crops and raise animals, and keep earning while you're away.

What we care about

  • Instant play. Every game loads fast and is built for mobile first.
  • Your privacy. No sign-up. Scores and progress are stored only in your own browser. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Free for everyone. Every game is free to play.
