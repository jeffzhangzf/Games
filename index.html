About Mini Games

Short on time, big on play. Mini Games is a small, hand-picked collection of free browser games you can start in seconds - no download, no sign-up, no install. Drop and merge numbers on your way to 2048, out-think an AI in a classic folk board game, rebuild pixel-art blueprints brick by brick, pop balloons against the clock, discover what kind of gamer you are, or plant a farm that keeps earning while you are away.

Puzzle games - for players who like to think one move ahead: Number Merge, Brick Builder and Two Beat One. Quick games - got one to five minutes? Balloon Pop, a round of Number Merge, or the one-minute Gamer Personality Quiz. Idle games - games that reward you for coming back, starting with Idle Farm, which earns coins while you are away.

Every game runs right here in your browser, on phone or desktop, and remembers your best scores and progress on your own device - so you can leave mid-game and pick up where you left off, without ever creating an account.

  • 📲Nothing to install. Every game plays in the browser, on mobile or desktop.
  • 🙅No account, ever. Scores and progress save on your device, not on a server.
  • 🎯No interruptions. No popups and no sign-up prompts - the game is the page.
  • ⏱️Short sessions welcome. Most games are built for a one-to-five-minute break.

FAQs

Are the games really free?

Yes. Every game on Mini Games is free to play in your browser, with no download, no sign-up and no payment.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There are no accounts. Your best scores and game progress are saved on your own device, in your browser.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The site and every game are built mobile-first and work in a phone browser - nothing to install from an app store.

Which game should I try first?

For a quick break, Balloon Pop or the Gamer Personality Quiz. To settle in, Number Merge or Idle Farm. Not sure? Take the quiz - it will tell you what kind of gamer you are.
