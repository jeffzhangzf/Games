Idle Games

This is a small category today, and we would rather say so than pad it. Its anchor is Idle Farm, a true idle game: plant wheat, carrots and pumpkins, raise chickens, cows and bees, and collect offline earnings every time you return. Alongside it, Number Merge suits the same longer, return-to-it session - a best score on each difficulty, waiting to be beaten. This category will grow as new idle games are added; for now, these are the games for players who like progress that waits for them.

FAQs

What is an idle game?

A game that progresses - and earns - while you are away.

Which idle games are here?

Idle Farm is the dedicated idle game; Number Merge is included as a longer-session game.

Does Idle Farm need the tab open?

No.

Will more idle games be added?

That is the plan for this category - check back.
